# Roadmap

<!-- Three rules, and the whole format collapses if any is broken.
     1. No dates. Not in the column headings, not in the rows, not as quarter
        labels, not as "early next year". A date is read as a commitment by
        everyone downstream and you will be held to an estimate you never made.
     2. Initiatives, not features. A row is a problem being solved with a
        measurable outcome. "Add CSV export" is a feature; "customers can get
        their data out without asking support" is an initiative.
     3. Detail scales with proximity. Now is specific because it is understood.
        Later is deliberately coarse because pretending otherwise is false
        precision. -->
