# Stakeholder Map

<!-- Purpose: know before you start who can approve this, who can stop it, and
     who has to live with it. Roles, not just names — but a map with no names
     at all is a template, not a map. -->

## Power / interest grid

<!-- Place each stakeholder by how much power they have over this work and how
     much interest they have in it. The quadrant determines how you engage, so
     misplacement is expensive.
     High power / high interest = manage closely.
     High power / low interest = keep satisfied; they will not read your doc but
     can still veto it.
     Low power / high interest = keep informed; they are your best source of
     detail.
     Low power / low interest = monitor.
     Be honest about power. Formal title and actual influence differ, and
     writing down the polite version of the org chart makes this useless. -->

| Stakeholder (name / role) | Power | Interest | Quadrant | Engagement approach |
