## Risks

<!-- Things that might happen and would hurt if they did. Write the risk as a
     cause-and-effect sentence, not a topic: not "security", but "the review
     queue is two weeks deep, so approval may land after the build is done and
     force rework".
     Rate likelihood and impact independently and multiply for severity —
     a low-likelihood, catastrophic-impact risk deserves a mitigation, and
     collapsing to one score hides that.
     Mitigation is what you do to reduce likelihood or impact. Contingency is
     what you do if it happens anyway. They are different, and a row with only
     a mitigation has no fallback.
