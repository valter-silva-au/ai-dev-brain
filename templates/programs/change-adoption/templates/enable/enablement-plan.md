## Evaluation

<!-- Four levels, and only the last two matter much:
     1. Reaction — did they find it useful? Cheap, weakly predictive.
     2. Learning — can they demonstrate it?
     3. Behaviour — are they doing it in real work weeks later?
     4. Results — did the capability gap actually close?
     Plan at least one level-3 check with a date. Attendance and satisfaction
     scores are not evidence of capability, and a plan that stops at level 1
     cannot tell you whether the gap closed. -->
