# Change Management Plan

<!-- Purpose: plan the human half of the change with the same rigour as the
     technical half.
     The model's usefulness is its ordering. The elements are sequential: no
     amount of training (Knowledge) helps someone who does not want the change
     (Desire), and no amount of desire helps someone who has not heard about it
     (Awareness). Diagnose which element is actually missing before choosing an
     intervention — most failed rollouts are Desire problems answered with
     training. -->

## Readiness by ADKAR element

<!-- Rate each affected group on each element, then let the lowest score choose
     the intervention.
     Awareness — do they know why this change is happening?
     Desire — do they want to participate? This is the one that cannot be
     assessed from the outside. Assuming it produces plans that train people
     who have already decided not to adopt, and the rollout gets reported as
     complete while usage sits at zero. Ask them.
     Knowledge — do they know how to change?
     Ability — can they actually do it in their real environment, under real
     pressure?
     Reinforcement — is there anything sustaining it after launch week? -->

## Sponsorship

<!-- Who is sponsoring the change, and what they have specifically agreed to

     it is the single strongest predictor of whether the change sticks. -->
