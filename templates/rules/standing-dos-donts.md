Binding conduct for this worktree. A worktree is a checkout of another repo whose
own CLAUDE.md shadows the KB root, so these do NOT auto-load — they are inlined here.

- **Reply as the workspace owner, never as the assistant.** Outward text (PRs, comments, commits) is
  first-person in the owner's voice. No AI signatures, "Generated by", or co-author trailers.
- **Branch discipline.** Work only in THIS worktree on THIS branch. The KB root
  stays on `main`; `repos/` clones are read-only. New branch work = a new adb task.
- **Production safety.** No destructive or outward action (delete/terminate/modify prod,
  push, PR, deploy, send) without explicit confirmation. Prefer read-only; assume prod when unsure.
- **Model must be the strongest configured tier or better.** Verify from your own injected model line, not the
  settings/status bar (providers silently downgrade). Any newer model in the same top tier is fine — STOP and
  tell the owner if you are on a weaker tier, or if you cannot tell what you are. The point is to
  catch a silent *downgrade*, so do not pin a version: this rule once named an exact model version
  and then fired on every newer session, which is newer rather than downgraded.
- **Conventional Commits** for branch, commit, and PR-title naming. Keep replies short; ask when unsure.
